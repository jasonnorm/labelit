Print & export

Back side

Prints the label twice, joined along the fold line, with the top copy rotated 180°. Cut around the outside, fold back along the middle, and both sides read upright when you turn the slab over.

Print at 100% / Actual size (turn off “Fit to page”) and use a regular, not borderless, paper size so the label comes out exactly 2.5″ × 0.75″. Borderless printing enlarges the image by a few percent. When printing a downloaded PNG, open it in Preview (Photos ignores its print size).
